Design Contract Agreement

This Agreement ("Agreement"), made and entered into this {{agreement_date}} and between Noble Solid Service DBA NSS Home, regarding the property at {{property_address}} ("Property")

WHEREAS, Homeowner desires to retain the services of Design-Builder and Design-Builder desires to provide such services to the Homeowner under the terms sets forward in this Agreement.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the respective rights and obligations hereinafter set forth, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Project Description. Design-Builder shall provide design services, including architectural, engineering, and other design professional services required by this agreement (the "Design"). Such design services shall be provided through qualified design professionals who are either employed by Design-Builder, or procured by Design-Builder from independent sources. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to create any legal or contractual relationship between Homeowner and any independent design professional.

2. Scope of Project. The scope of this design project shall be as detailed in the attached Project Addendum (the "Scope"). Design-Builder will provide design services for the above described remodeling project. If Homeowner changes the Scope of the design after this initial Agreement, additional design and consultation fees will be charged. The revised Scope, including any additional design and consultation fees, will be set forth in a written change order signed by all parties. As part of the Scope, Design-Builder will often conduct a feasibility study if necessary. The purpose of the feasibility study is to identify items that might increase cost. Design-Builder will only inspect what can be seen or discovered via visual inspection without compromising integrity or aesthetic of structure. If further exploration is prudent, and Homeowner agrees/requests, a small project contract and a service fee may be required.

On this day of august 2023 proposed design element(s), corresponding production liability protections, warranties and/or any other implied formal or informal written or uttered assurances relating to Design-Builder's Design are considered null and void if Homeowner opts to employ another Builder to construct the Design. Homeowner will not hold Design-Builder liable for use of these plans by any third party not designated by Design-Builder, or should the client decide to hire any other party or independent contractor to complete the work at any point after the Design Services are delivered complete and Homeowner waives any and all such claims against Design-Builder.

3. Contract Price. Price, payment amounts, and payment milestones are as outlined in scope notes addendum, attached at the top of this contact.

4. Preliminary Services by Homeowner and Homeowner Provided Information. Homeowner shall provide Design-Builder with Homeowner's Project Criteria describing Homeowner's requirements and objectives regarding the project. Homeowner shall give Design-Builder accurate information about the project, including, but not limited to, use, space, time, site, and performance requirements. Further, if applicable, Homeowner shall provide Design-Builder with any surveys, geotechnical studies, easements, drawings, and environmental studies of the site. When performing its obligations, Design-Builder is entitled to relay on the information provided by Homeowner. Homeowner agrees to communicate with Design-Builder throughout the process using the Buildertrend project management website.

5. Design Documents. Design-Builder shall prepare design documents based on the Homeowner's Project criteria (the "Design Documents"). The Design Documents may include drawings, diagrams, specifications, engineering recommendations and a fixed price setting forth the requirements of the project. Renderings that are produced in the Design Phase are Artistic Interpretations and are subject to change. In addition, all dimensions given are subject to verification and adjustment during the build phase.

6. Selections by the Homeowner. During the Design Project, Homeowner may be required to make final selections. Homeowner agrees to make all selection for the Property when requested to do so by Design-Builder. Homeowner's selections will be posted to Buildertrend by Design-Builder. If Homeowner wants to make changes to Homeowner's selections or floor plans after the Finalization Date under this Section 6 has expired, a signed, written change order will be necessary. Further, after the Finalization Date under this Section 6 has expired for Homeowner's selections, Design-Builder shall have sole and absolute discretion to approve or disapprove of any selection by Homeowner. Homeowner agrees that failure to make selection by the Finalization Date may delay the presentation of any Build Proposal, and, as applicable, the start of the build phase (if a Build Contract is agreed to), along with extending the job length and adding cost to Homeowner's Project. Design-Builder shall not be responsible for any delays due to Homeowner's time in picking out the selections necessary for the project.

7. Build Proposal.
a. Based on Homeowner's Project Criteria, the Design Documents, and any other relevant information upon which the parties may agree, Design-Builder may, in its sole discretion submit a proposal to Homeowner (the "Build Proposal"), which shall include the following unless the parties mutually agree otherwise:
i. A proposed contract price for the construction of the project in the form of a total fixed price bid
ii. The time limit for acceptance of the Build Proposal
iii. The detailed intended project scope
b. If Design-Builder seeks to enter into a contract to construct the improvements, it is the Design-Builder's practice to provide a total fixed price bid and not provide an itemized quote. Instead, the Design-Builder provides a comprehensive and detailed project scope at a total fixed price at the end of the design and feasibility process. For reasons of timeliness, efficiency, warranty and thoroughness, Design-Builder does not allow Homeowner to purchase their own selections other than appliances (or, if agreed upon by Design-Builder, some unique item that is difficult to procure). Design-Builder provides a 5-year warranty on all the work and materials that it provides as a part of the contract to build.

8. Review of Proposal and Completion of This Agreement. If Homeowner finds any inconsistencies or inaccuracies in the information presented in the Build Proposal, it shall promptly notify the Design-Builder in writing of such inconsistencies. If Design-Builder finds the revisions acceptable, Design-Builder shall adjust the Build Proposal. Design-Builder's services under this Agreement shall be deemed completed when Design-Builder has completed the Design Documents and Homeowner has designated their selections for the design. The decision about whether the Design-Builder will submit a Build Proposal is left to the complete discretion of Design-Builder.

9. Hidden Damages or Concealed Areas. In the event Design-Builder and Homeowner enter into a contract to build the design, any costs associated with the repair or remediation of hidden damages and undiscoverable conditions are not included in the Build Proposal. Hidden damages and undiscoverable conditions may include a hidden or concealed condition not discovered or evident without altering space or structure or more invasive measures during the estimating process, including, but not limited to, wood rot, insect damage, or hazardous material removal. Moreover, hidden damages and undiscoverable conditions can result in governmental organizations requiring compliance with coding rules before a project can proceed.

10. Signs & Marketing. Homeowner will initial here__________ if they allow Design-Builder to place a small job-site sign and brochure box on property. Homeowner will initial here__________ if they do not allow Design-Builder to place a small job-site sign and brochure box on property.

11. Work Product. All drawings, specifications and other documents and electronic data furnished by Design-Builder to Homeowner under this Agreement are deemed to be instruments of service and Design-Builder shall retain the ownership and property interests therein, including, but not limited, to any intellectual property rights, copyrights and/or patents, subjects to the provisions set forth in Section 12.

12. Homeowners' Election to Not Use Design-Builder. If, after Design-Builder submits a Build Proposal, Homeowner or Design-Builder decide not to enter into a contract on this project and Homeowner proceeds to construct the project through its employees, agents or third parties, Design-Builder, upon payment in full of the amounts due Design-Builder under this Agreement, transfers to Homeowner all Homeownership and property interests, including, but not limited to, intellectual property rights, copyrights, in the Work Product. Such transfer is conditioned on the following:
a. Homeowner's Limited License. Use of the work product under section 11 is at Homeowner's sole risk without liability or legal exposure to Design-Builder or anyone working by or through Design-Builder, including design consultants of any tier (collectively the "Indemnified Parties". Homeowner shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the Indemnified Parties from and against any and all claims, damages, liabilities, losses and expenses, including attorneys' fees, arising out of or resulting from the use of the Work Project.

13. Choice of Law and Venue. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with the laws of Washington State with giving effect to the Washington State conflict of law provisions. The Parties further agree that the location and jurisdiction for any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be proper only in any federal or state court located only in Pierce County, Washington State.

14. Notice. All notices, demands and requests which may be given, or which are required to be given by either Party of a legal nature, to the other Party must be in writing, unless otherwise specifically provided for in this Agreement, and shall be sent by express, registered or certified mail, return receipt request or with delivery confirmation, postage prepaid, by overnight courier service such as Federal Express, by facsimile transmission or by personal delivery and addressed to each Party.

15. Dispute Resolution. The parties agree to make contact and to attempt to resolve any dispute privately within ten (10) days after an issue occurs.

16. Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the Homeowner and Design-Builder relating to the subject matter hereof. The Parties shall not be bound by or liable for any statement, writing, representation, promise, inducement or understanding not set forth herein or in and Exhibit hereto.

17. Voluntary Signature. The Parties represent that each has carefully read this Agreement, that he knows and understand the contents and consequences thereof, and that he has signed this Agreement voluntarily and with informed consent. In executing this Agreement, Homeowner and Design-Builder each individually represents that it has the necessary financial resources to fulfill its obligations under this Agreement, and each has the necessary corporate approvals to execute this Agreement, and perform the services described herein.
